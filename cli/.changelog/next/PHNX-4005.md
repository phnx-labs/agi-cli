- **AGI Menu palette: dispatch form with remembered defaults, project pulse, and session-id minting (PHNX-4005).**
  The `Cmd-Shift-O` palette now carries the dimensions a dispatch actually needs
  below its text field — **mode** (Plan / Auto / Edit), **run on** (This Mac,
  `auto`, or a fleet device by name), **surface** (Interactive / Headless), and
  **watchdog** (Off / Keep moving / Hands-off) — collapsed to one summary line
  (`Claude 2.1.263 · this-mac · agi · Auto · Interactive · Keep moving`) with a
  `▸ details` disclosure. Every dimension is **remembered per project**, so
  switching projects restores the agent, mode, and target you last used there and
  one keystroke dispatches. `Cmd-P` switches **this one dispatch** to Plan without
  touching the remembered defaults. A Claude dispatch mints its session id and
  passes `--session-id`, which lets the palette apply a non-default watchdog policy
  at dispatch and register a **launching placeholder** with the feed stream: the
  row shows in the project pulse strip as `◌ launching · <name>` (and in any
  window that reads the feed rows) until the real session reports, and reads
  "did not start" — with the CLI's own stderr — if the launch exits non-zero or
  no row arrives within 60 s. Under the project row a **pulse strip** shows the
  headline milestone from `agents projects status`, open tickets and PRs, live
  running / need-you / idle counts with NOW chips (not-progressing first), and
  the NEXT UP ticket (`Cmd-N` attaches).
