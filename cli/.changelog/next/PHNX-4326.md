- **Resumed sessions stay live in `agents sessions --active` and the feed (PHNX-4326).**
  An unattributed row was stamped with its transcript's birthtime, so a `claude --resume`
  of an older conversation failed the pid-reuse guard on every cached tick and dropped
  to Previous/closed while it was running. The row now carries its process start.
  Source: `src/lib/session/active.ts`.
- **Cloud tasks nothing has confirmed for a day no longer show as working (PHNX-4326).**
  A `queued`/`allocating`/`running` task whose last update is over 24 hours old is left
  out of the active list and the feed; `agents cloud list` still asks the provider about
  every unsettled task. Source: `src/lib/cloud/store.ts`.
- **`agents sessions preview --json` carries `subAgents` and `images`, and `todos` folds
  Claude's task tools (PHNX-4326).** `subAgents` lists each spawned subagent with its
  status and duration, `images` the last six images the session was given, read or
  wrote that still exist on disk, and `todos` now includes `TaskCreate`/`TaskUpdate`
  checklists. See `docs/menubar.md`. Source: `src/commands/sessions-picker.ts`,
  `src/lib/session/glance-files.ts`.
