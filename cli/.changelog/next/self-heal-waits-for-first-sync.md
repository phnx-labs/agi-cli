- **The daemon no longer installs hooks and permissions on a fresh install before you choose them.** `agents setup` starts the daemon, and its self-heal pass ran about 30 seconds later, while `agents add` was still showing the first-run prompt. It saw a version with nothing synced, called every resource missing, and installed all of them, so the hooks and auto-approved commands the prompt leaves unchecked were already in place whatever you picked. Self-heal now skips a version until its first sync has recorded what you chose, and repairs it as before after that. Source: `cli/src/lib/heal.ts`.
