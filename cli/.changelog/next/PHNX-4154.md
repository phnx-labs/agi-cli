  `session_text` FTS rows now sit at the rowid of the `sessions` row they describe and are
  addressed by it (schema v51, a one-time rebuild: 24 s for a 669 MB index). Every delete and
  content read used to filter on the UNINDEXED `session_id`, a full scan of the FTS content per
  statement; the fleet session mirror runs one per mirrored row inside a daemon tick, so the
  daemon's event loop stalled for 10+ minutes per tick, every service logged `tick exceeded
  deadline`, and `agents browser` verbs on the same loop timed out with
  `Arc Apple Event timed out`. Source: `cli/src/lib/session/db.ts`.
