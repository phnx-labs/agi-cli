- **A session row says whether its last turn was done, and a finished turn posts a banner.** Live rows on `agents ps --json`, `agents ps watch --json` and `agents feed watch --json` (remote rows too) carry `doneVerdict: { state: 'done'|'open'|'unchecked', gaps, at }` from the Stop hook's verdict in `~/.agents/.cache/state/sessions/<pid>.json`, only when that record belongs to the row's live process. When an interactive session goes from running to idle after at least 2 minutes of work, AGI Menu posts one `done` banner: "✓ Done", "Not done" or "Finished" with the row's title, the verdict's gaps in words when it is open or else the first sentence of the agent's last line, and a tap opens the session. One banner per turn; three or more finishes that land together post one summary banner. The `menubar.menu.notifyNormal` schedule governs it (`sidebar` turns it off).
