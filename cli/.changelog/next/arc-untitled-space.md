- **An untitled Arc Space no longer hides every Arc Space from `agents browser`.** `parseSidebarSpaces` treated the display-only Space title as part of a Space's identity and rejected the whole sidebar when one Space had none, so `agents browser start` warned that a configured `arc-gmail` "can't launch on this machine" while `agents browser profiles doctor arc-gmail` saw Arc and the Space. Identity is the stable Space id and its profile binding; a Space Arc stored without a title (key absent or `null`) is now listed as `arc-space` (two or more get their Space-id prefix appended, as same-titled Spaces already do). A mismatched id, a duplicate id, an unknown or malformed profile mapping, and a title that is present but not text still fail discovery closed. Source: `cli/src/lib/browser/arc-discovery.ts`, `cli/src/lib/browser/arc-discovery.test.ts`, `cli/src/lib/browser/testdata/arc/`.
