- **Antigravity runs headless with JSON, resume, plan, effort and extra directories, and picks only a login it will actually use.** `agents run antigravity "…" --json` passes `agy --output-format stream-json` (the old TODO said it was broken upstream; agy 1.5.16 streams `init`, `step_update` and `result` events), and `agents teams` now parses those events into messages, `bash`, `file_create`/`file_write` and results instead of `unknown`. `--resume <id>` maps to `agy --conversation <id>` (agy 1.1.27+). `--mode plan` maps to `agy --mode plan` and runs read-only headless, so it no longer degrades to a writable run; `--mode edit` maps to `agy --mode accept-edits`. `-e/--effort` passes `agy --effort <level>` and `--add-dir` passes `agy --add-dir`, where both were silently dropped. Under `--strategy balanced`, an Antigravity version counted as signed in when its own home held a token, but agy reads whichever home `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli` points at, so the run asked for a Google login and timed out after 60 s. A symlink-adopted harness (antigravity, droid, goose and the others without a config env) now counts a version as launchable only while the symlink names its home; `agents use antigravity@<version>` switches it. The `agy` preset no longer claims a gemini-3.5-flash default, and `prices.json` adds Gemini 4 Carbon, Argon, Neon and Hydrogen, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.1 Pro at Google's list prices after the introductory prices end (2026-12-01 for Carbon and Argon, 2027-01-01 for 3.8 Flash).
