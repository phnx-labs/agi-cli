- **Remote sessions have one execution-owned row (PHNX-3939).** Fleet session and feed streams reconcile launcher observations with the worker's state, retaining terminal/viewing metadata separately. Reconnects and stale history cannot replace a genuine worker row. Registry cleanup retains records whose PID namespace cannot be verified; nested agent runs keep launch lineage without claiming their parent's editor terminal.
- **Codex forks retain their child identity.** Full and incremental scans keep the first native metadata record. Reindexing repairs cold files and removes a stale parent binding to child bytes even when the parent rollout is absent. Team descendants also keep their immediate parent lineage without inheriting its editor ownership.
- **Nested daemon starts preserve shared session state.** Legacy migration and reboot recovery require the initial host namespace or kernel-verified live daemon ownership before touching singleton state. Foreign snapshots remain read-only, and invisible processes are never treated as dead. Automatic reuse of a private-container HOME across namespaces is unsupported, including after the prior namespace exits; writers fail safely with a diagnostic.
