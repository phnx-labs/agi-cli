- **`agents computer --device` and `agents secrets --device` forward the standalone engines' own `--host` address (PHNX-4090).** `computer`, `secrets`, and `browser` are extracted CLIs with no fleet registry of their own (PHNX-4075, PHNX-3989); they share one address grammar (`ssh://[user@]host`, `vnc://host[:port]`, `tcp://host:port`, or a bare `user@host`/alias) via a copied `lib/address.ts`. A device's `computer.host` config (`agents config set devices.<name>.computer.host <address>`, next to `defaultBrowserProfile`) selects the transport — `vnc://`/`tcp://` for a non-Windows desktop, or `ssh://` still resolved against the fleet identity; with none set, `--device` falls back to the historical Windows-only ssh tunnel. `agents computer`'s `withDeviceFlag` is gone, replaced by `withHostFlag` + `resolveDeviceHost`; `agents secrets`'s passthrough gained `rewriteDeviceToHost`. An explicit `--host` on the command line always wins over `--device`; `agents view --device <name>` is unaffected — it still SSHes the whole command. Source: `src/lib/address.ts`, `src/commands/computer.ts`, `src/commands/secrets-passthrough.ts`, `src/lib/device-config.ts`, `src/lib/config-keys.ts`.
