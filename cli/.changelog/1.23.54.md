- **`agents recordings` keeps its ledger under `~/.agents/.history/recordings/`.** It wrote `config.json` and `ledger.json` to `~/.agents/recordings/`, the root of your tracked resources repo, so they showed up as untracked files there. An existing `~/.agents/recordings/` is moved on first use, so already-published recordings keep their rows and are not uploaded again. Source: `cli/src/lib/recordings/config.ts`.
